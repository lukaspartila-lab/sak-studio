Privacy Policy – Sak Studio Pin Scheduler

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Sak Studio Pin Scheduler ("the App") is a private tool operated by Sak Studio (SakShopByArty on Etsy, sakshop.gumroad.com). It is used only by the shop owner to publish Pins of Sak Studio's own products to the owner's own Pinterest boards and to read the owner's own boards and analytics.

Information we access

  • Only the shop owner's own Pinterest account data needed for the App to work: boards, Pins and Pin/board analytics.
  • The App does not collect, access or store any data about other Pinterest users or visitors.

How we use it

  • To create and schedule Pins for our own products and to review their performance.

Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely in a private, access-restricted environment and are never published or shared.
  • No Pinterest passwords or session cookies are collected.

Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

Your control

  • Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings (Security and logins / Apps). Stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Questions: send a message via our Etsy shop (SakShopByArty) or our Gumroad shop (sakshop.gumroad.com).

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.
